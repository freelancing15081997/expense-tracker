Delete Byjan data without deleting your account

This page is for the Byjan app (EasyPado / package com.byjanbooks.app).

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open Byjan and sign in.
  2. Open a money book you can edit.
  3. Delete individual entries, receipts, or (if you own it) the whole book.

That removes those records from your workspace right away.

By email (for a broader wipe)

  1. Email byjanbooks@gmail.com from your Byjan sign-in address.
  2. Use the subject Byjan data deletion.
  3. Say what to remove (for example: all receipts, one book, or your display name) and that you want to keep the account.

We will confirm and complete the request within 30 days.

What is deleted vs kept

  • Deleted: the entries, books, receipts, or profile fields you asked us to remove.
  • Kept: your login so you can still sign in, plus any shared-book rows owned by other members.
  • Security backups of deleted items, if any, are dropped within 90 days.

To close the account entirely, use Delete your Byjan account. Privacy policy: privacy.html
